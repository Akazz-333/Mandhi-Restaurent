About Mandhi House

Heritage • slow subterranean smoke • fire craft

The Legacy of Slow Underground Smoke

Mandhi House is born from a deep reverence for the ancient culinary methods of Yemen. In the historical kitchens of Hadramout, cooking was a sacred chemical dance of earth, clay, embers, and time. We bring that exact legacy to Cochin.

Our signature Mandi and Madfoon dishes are prepared inside hand-crafted clay-lined underground pits. Suspended directly over glowing charcoal embers, the meats roast slowly in their own juices. As they cook, the fat drippings are caught by a giant cauldron of long-grain basmati rice sitting below, infusing every single grain with rich, savory wood smoke, ghee, and natural juices.

Every seasoning is selected by hand and ground daily—from the minty capsules of Green Cardamom to the dried black limes (Loomi) imported to retain the citric, honest spirit of Aden.
